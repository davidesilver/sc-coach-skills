# Periodization Block Planner

## When to activate this skill
Together with `elite-sc-system` and before generating any microcycle with `programming-audit-council`, when you need to decide the macro structure (weeks/months) before drilling down into the detail of a single week.

**Evidence tags.** `[Source Year, doi, grade]`; grades VERIFIED, UNVERIFIABLE, OPINION (coaching practice), C (internal rule). Audit: 2026-10-08. The %1RM, rep and set ranges below are coaching practice [OPINION]. The phase durations attributed to Issurin could not be checked: the open abstracts give none [UNVERIFIABLE].

## The Block model: three phases, not three opinions
Block periodization divides the macrocycle into three sequential phases with distinct, non-overlapping physiological goals.

### Phase 1 — Accumulation
- **Goal**: build work volume and base capacity, not test strength.
- **Intensity**: 60-75% of estimated/actual 1RM.
- **Reps**: 6-12 per set (some protocols go up to 20 for specific hypertrophy work).
- **Rest**: longer rests build more strength and muscle: "longer rest periods promote greater increases in muscle strength and hypertrophy" (3 min vs 1 min, trained men) [Schoenfeld 2016, doi:10.1519/JSC.0000000000001272, abstract, VERIFIED]. Use 30-90 s only to save time [OPINION].
- **Typical duration**: 2-6 weeks.
- Work is mostly on accessory/bodybuilding-style movements at high volume, not on the big 3 as the absolute priority.

### Phase 2 — Intensification / Transmutation
- **Goal**: convert accumulated volume into real strength, increasing the load on the bar.
- **Intensity**: 75-90% of 1RM.
- **Reps**: 3-6 per set, 3-4 sets.
- **Rest**: longer, to allow neural recovery and ATP resynthesis between heavy sets.
- **Typical duration**: 2-4 weeks, the most mentally and physically demanding phase of the block.
- This is where strength is built — the max is not yet tested here.

### Phase 3 — Realization / Peaking
- **Goal**: express the accumulated strength/power in competition or testing, minimizing residual fatigue.
- **Intensity**: 85-95%+ of 1RM.
- **Reps**: 1-3 per set, long rest periods (2-5 minutes).
- **Typical duration**: about 2 weeks. "A 2-wk taper during which training volume is exponentially reduced by 41-60%", intensity and frequency unchanged [Bosquet 2007, doi:10.1249/mss.0b013e31806010e0, abstract, VERIFIED, endurance athletes]. For strength athletes the "optimal taper duration may only be 2 weeks", up to 4 [Travis 2020, doi:10.3390/sports8090125, full text, VERIFIED].

## Summary table of parameters by phase

| Phase | %1RM | Reps | Sets | Rest | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accumulation | 60-75% | 6-12(-20) | 3-5 | 60-180s | 2-6 wks |
| Intensification | 75-90% | 3-6 | 3-4 | 90-180s | 2-4 wks |
| Realization | 85-95%+ | 1-3 | 2-3 | 2-5min | ~2 wks |

## When to choose Block vs. Linear vs. DUP vs. Conjugate
- **Block**: intermediate/advanced athletes with a single clear peak (competition, test, season); maximum phase specificity.
- **Linear**: beginners, where every variable can progress predictably without needing undulation.
- **DUP (Daily Undulating)**: athletes who need to maintain multiple qualities (strength+hypertrophy+power) in parallel year-round — typical of team sports in-season.
- **Conjugate**: advanced athletes/powerlifters who need to stimulate max effort, dynamic effort, and the repetition method in the same week without ever stalling.

## Off-Season → In-Season Transition
- **Off-season**: 10-20 sets/muscle group, full block periodization (accumulation→intensification→realization).
- **In-season**: MED (minimum effective dose) 4-8 sets/muscle group, ≥80% 1RM, priority on maintenance rather than aggressive progression — consistent with `elite-sc-system`'s load rules.
- **Transition rule**: never enter the season during an accumulation phase; the last phase before the season starts must be realization/peaking, otherwise the athlete arrives at week 1 of the season carrying residual fatigue.

## Progression rule (never by calendar)
[C, internal rule] Do not advance phase unless all 3 conditions are met: actual RPE ≤ target+0.5 on at least 3 main sets, no pain check ≥3/10, average sleep ≥7h. This rule is binding and consistent with the Weekly Continuity Check in `programming-audit-council`.
